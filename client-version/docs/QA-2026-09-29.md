## Boundaries and follow-up

- OCR extraction is document-dependent. The negative photo test verifies a missing document is rejected; it does not establish document authenticity or a guaranteed recognition rate for real-world identities.
- The payment invoice acknowledges uploaded evidence while independent staff review remains pending. External activation is recorded by backend staff after their separate workflow.
- Some older browser specs still assert retired field tasks, team views, or former receipt fixtures. They need maintenance against the owner workflow; their failure is not evidence that the current flow is implemented. Run the current hosted checks separately so Playwright runs do not share an output directory.
